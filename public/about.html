Our mission

Turn a project’s scattered files into one expert that has read everything, so every answer can be traced to its source.

Sila started on site, on a large GCC residential project, where the answer to almost every question existed and finding it took hours. We are not building another platform to file into. We are building the colleague who has read it all.

The name comes from silsila, a chain. Sila, صلة, is the link: every record on a project answers, replaces or repeats another. Sila follows those links.
