How it works

Keep every system you have. Sila reads across them.

Aconex, Unifier and Procore store and manage records. Sila doesn’t compete with them. It is the expert that reads what is in them, and in everything around them.

Your people ask, in English or Arabic

Site engineersDocument controlQA/QCProject managersQS and contractsMEP coordinatorsPlanners

Sila reads, links, answers and flags

Read-only. It builds one map of how your records relate: which revision replaced which, which RFI answered which clash, which copy is behind.

Your project stays where it lives. Nothing moves.

DMS and CDE platformsShared serversCloud foldersEmailPrimavera P6Subcontractor and supplier packagesScans, CAD, Excel

Sila is

  • An expert on your project that has read every file, wherever it is stored
  • A checker that tells you what is missing before work starts
  • A quiet watcher that flags problems nobody asked about
  • A builder of trackers, schedules and chronologies from scattered records

Sila is not

  • A document management system, or a replacement for one
  • A new place to file, or a migration project
  • A chatbot you paste files into
  • A replacement for your document controller or engineers
What it answers

Nine kinds of question. All with sources.

Find documents

The latest revision, the right copy, and scopes filed inside another trade’s package.

Status and can-we-proceed

Return codes, what is open or overdue, and whether you can pour, fabricate or install.

Readiness checks

Everything an activity needs, and where the approved record departs from IFC.

Drawing consistency

Related drawings checked against each other: titles, location references, levels and datums.

Change impact

Everything a change touches, across trades, to where the services path ends.

Values and quantities

Sizes, levels, cover and counts read from drawings, for QS and BOQ checks.

History, NCRs and claims

Who held an item and for how long, and the evidence behind a delay or claim.

Trackers and registers

Logs, schedules and weekly digests, built as Excel from scattered records.

Project reference data

Registers of plots, floors, zones and rooms; unit types, levels and setting-out.

Company memory

Lessons carried from one project to the next, so the next job starts smarter.
