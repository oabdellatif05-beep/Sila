Trust

Built to be checked.

Read-only

Sila reads your systems. It never edits, moves or deletes a file.

Sources on every answer

Each answer points to the document, revision and page it came from.

Says when it can’t find it

No guessing. It tells you what it could not find and where it looked.

Do we have to replace Aconex, Unifier or Procore?

No. Sila is not a DMS and does not store your records. It reads from the systems you already run, plus the shared servers, inboxes and subcontractor and supplier packages those systems do not cover.

Do we need to clean up or migrate our files first?

No. Messy servers, scanned approvals and inconsistent file names are the starting point. Your document controller reviews the setup before go-live.

What if Sila gets an answer wrong?

Every answer shows its sources, so anyone can check it in seconds. Where records conflict, Sila reports both and says which is newer; it does not pick one unless an instruction or RFI settles it.

Is our data used to train AI models?

Your project data is never used to train models for anyone else. Hosting region: [to confirm].

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Ask in Arabic, English or a mix. Sources stay in their original language.

Does it replace our document controller?

No. It takes the “can you find me” requests off their desk. Filing, transmittals and control of the record stay with your team.

Start with one project.

Eight weeks, read-only, measured against your own team. Then roll out, extend or stop.
